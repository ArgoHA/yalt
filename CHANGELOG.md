## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-14

### Added

- Fully local object-detection, instance-segmentation, and image-classification
  projects for macOS.
- YOLO and COCO detection and polygon import/export.
- Folder and CSV classification workflows.
- Project-local SQLite autosave, recovery, and undo/redo history.
- JPEG, PNG, WebP, and TIFF indexing and display.
- Retina canvas, trackpad navigation, keyboard shortcuts, and accessible UI
  controls.
